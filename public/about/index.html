About WILS Research

Research support should make sense to you.

WILS Research is an analytical team that helps students and professionals design better projects, carry out sound analysis, prepare clear presentations and develop academic or professional documents they can confidently use. We focus on work that makes sense to the client—not just work that looks complete.

Explore servicesTalk with our team

WILS Research is a BRELA-registered business name (Registration No. 635846, registered 15 March 2026). We work under agreed scope, ethical handling of data, transparent communication and respect for confidentiality.

From the founder

Ramadhan Majid

Founder & CEO, WILS Research

“My name is Ramadhan Majid, and I created WILS Research to help students and professionals do their very best work. Too often, people struggle alone with proposals, datasets, analysis and presentations. I believe research support should be thoughtful, cooperative and practical—so that the final product is not only completed, but also understandable, useful and something the client feels proud of.”

“With my experience, I believe deeply in hard work, discussion and working together. At WILS Research, we want to help you understand your project phases, identify challenges early, and build a clear path from idea to output. Our long-term vision is even broader: to contribute to medical, social, cultural and demographic research that helps society make better decisions based on data.”

“Welcome to WILS Research. Let us discuss your research, understand what you want to do, and plan how to create strong work together.”
