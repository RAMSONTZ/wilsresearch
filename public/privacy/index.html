Privacy and project information

We use the details you submit to respond to enquiries, discuss services and manage agreed work. Please avoid entering patient-identifiable information or confidential research data into the public contact forms.

Booking and contact forms

When you use the live booking form, WILS stores your name, WhatsApp number, requested service, scope notes, booking status, transaction reference, sender details and payment verification history in its protected booking database. Only authorized WILS personnel may access the full booking ledger. The admin confirms payments manually against the receiving account. The website never asks for your mobile-money PIN, bank password, ID document or card details.

Documents

For research datasets and sensitive documents, contact us to agree on an appropriate secure transfer method. Public enquiry forms do not provide a confidential research-data portal.

Deposits, priority and cancellations

The research-services base package is TZS 100,000, with a TZS 20,000 initial deposit and TZS 80,000 remaining on review of the agreed work. VIP adds an optional TZS 10,000 priority-handling charge, subject to availability. Group numbers are administrative batches of seven confirmed bookings and are not guaranteed delivery dates. Confirm the written scope, realistic timing, cancellation and refund arrangements with WILS before transferring money. If WILS cannot accept the work, contact WILS about the deposit and refund process.

Private booking links

Each booking has a private tracking token. Anyone holding that token and its reference can see the booking status, so do not share your tracking link publicly. WILS does not publish the full client queue.
